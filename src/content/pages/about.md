## Product-minded systems thinker. ##

I’ve always been drawn to creating things that make life easier, more enjoyable or simply more interesting.

After art school, launching a digital product and helping startups grow, I started learning to code in 2021 so I could **build things myself.**

In 2023, a *snowboarding accident* left me with a TBI and put that journey on hold. I kept going, rebuilding my computer tolerance from just a few minutes to multiple hours while continuing to learn and create.

Today, I build **thoughtful full-stack products** and explore how AI can turn complex information into useful, trustworthy guidance. 
Recent projects include [Daily Recovery Coach](../projects/daily-recovery-coach) born from my rehabilitation, and [MyPower](../projects/mypower), inspired by my love for *calisthenics*.

I enjoy untangling complex flows, refining UX and building products that genuinely help people. I’m especially interested in how AI is changing software development, from *AI-assisted coding* to building reliable **AI agents** and intelligent products.

**Open to front-end and full-stack roles, as well as focused freelance assignments.**
